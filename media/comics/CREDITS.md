# Comics: credits and license

All strips are **xkcd by Randall Munroe**, https://xkcd.com, licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution-NonCommercial 2.5 License (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/2.5/), as stated
on https://xkcd.com/license.html (checked 2026-09-29). They are used unmodified on this
non-commercial course site, each shown with its title, its alt text and a link to its permalink.
Titles, alt text and image URLs were taken from each strip's `https://xkcd.com/NNNN/info.0.json`;
the double-resolution image was downloaded where xkcd serves one.
