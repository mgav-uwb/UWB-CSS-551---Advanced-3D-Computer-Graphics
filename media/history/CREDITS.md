# History draft media: credits

Images named in sections B and D of the outline, downloaded on 2026-10-03 from the source the
catalog names and resized so the long side is at most 800 px (`sips -Z 800`). File names are
catalog ids. Commons licenses and authors were re-read from the Commons API on download.
Fair-use items are approved by Marcel for review, pending a UW copyright review; each carries
the label "Used under fair use for teaching and commentary."

Second pass (same day): paper figures from open copies (DTIC and archive.org scans, Cambridge,
Pixar's research library, Stanford, Debevec, Disney, arXiv sources, Caltech's repository, the
Wayback copy of Cornell's history page), each recorded with paper, figure and page in the
manifest; film stills and game screenshots from studio project pages or English Wikipedia
non-free files (original file, not a thumbnail); the HMD photo (paper Fig. 2, TECHNES scan on
archive.org) and the RAND Tablet (RM-4122 Fig. 1, DTIC); and the Commons files the outline marks
"add", listed in ../../fair-use/catalog-additions.json under ids commons-*.
