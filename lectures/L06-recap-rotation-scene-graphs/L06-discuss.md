## Wrap

Every query about points, lines and planes is a dot, a cross or a sign, and the dot and the cross are the cosine and the sine of the angle between two directions. A rotation is where the axes land: Rodrigues turns the part across the axis like a 2D rotation, a quaternion stores axis and angle in four numbers that compose by multiplication.
