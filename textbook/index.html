CSS 551 · Course Text

Advanced 3D Computer Graphics · 25 topic chapters
Chapters are organized by topic, not by week. Each stands on its own, links the interactive demos it needs, and cites its papers. Your offering’s course site says which chapters go with which session.

Each chapter states its definitions, works every formula by hand at least once, draws its figures from the same library the demos run, carries hands-on tasks, names where each method fails with numbers, and ends with exercises and their solutions. Open a card to read the chapter; press ? for what it covers and what it assumes.
