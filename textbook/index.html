Advanced 3D Computer Graphics · topic chapters
Chapters are organized by topic, not by week. Each stands on its own, links the interactive demos it needs, and cites its papers. Your offering’s course site says which chapters go with which session.

Read the history first; it gives every later topic an address. The three foundations chapters (vectors, rotation, affine transformations) are the mathematics every later chapter stands on; the three pipeline chapters (scene graphs, viewing, rasterization) carry a vertex from a model file to a pixel; the three surface chapters (meshes, texture mapping, illumination) build the surface, dress it, and light it. The five that follow are the mathematics behind the course’s last two lectures: honest light, then the machinery of models that learn images and scenes. More chapters are being added in blocks of three. Each states its definitions, evaluates every formula by hand at least once in a worked example, and draws its figures from the same library the course’s demos run, so a figure and the demo beside it cannot disagree.
