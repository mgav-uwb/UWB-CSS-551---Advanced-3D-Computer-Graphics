Advanced 3D Computer Graphics — topic chapters
Chapters are organized by topic, not by week. Each stands on its own, links the interactive demos it needs, and cites its papers. Your offering’s course site says which chapters go with which session.

Read the history first; it gives every later topic an address. The five chapters that follow it are the mathematics behind the course’s last lecture, written out: honest light, then the machinery of models that learn images and scenes.
