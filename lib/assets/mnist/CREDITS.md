# MNIST Subset Credits

`mnist-2000-20x20.bin` (2,000 × 400 bytes) and `mnist-2000-labels.bin` (2,000 bytes) are a
class-balanced subset of the **MNIST** handwritten-digit training set: the first 200
images of each digit 0–9, interleaved by class, each 28×28 image cropped to the centered
20×20 box the digits occupy. `manifest.json` records the shape.

- **Source:** MNIST database of handwritten digits, Yann LeCun, Corinna Cortes and
  Christopher J.C. Burges; fetched from the CVDF mirror
  (`storage.googleapis.com/cvdf-datasets/mnist/`) on 2026-09-16.
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution-Share Alike 3.0 (CC BY-SA 3.0), as stated by the
  dataset's authors. Attribution is required; this derived subset is shared under the same
  license.
- **On-slide / in-demo credit line:** MNIST (LeCun, Cortes, Burges) · CC BY-SA 3.0
- **Generated by:** `node tools/prep-mnist.mjs <images.gz> <labels.gz> 200`. Regenerate with
  the tool; never hand-edit the binaries.

## Trained weights

`mlp-denoiser.bin` (1,002,064 float32 weights, 4.0 MB) and `mlp-denoiser.json` (layout
manifest) are the class-conditional MLP denoiser used by `lib/demos/diffusion-net.js`,
trained by `tools/train-mlp.py` on 2026-09-16 on all 60,000 MNIST training digits
(cropped to 20×20) for 900 s on a laptop CPU (41,210 steps, batch 512, AdamW, cosine
schedule s = 0.008, class dropout 15 % for classifier-free guidance, predicting the clean
image). A derived work of MNIST, shared under the same CC BY-SA 3.0. Regenerate with the
script from a venv holding `torch`, `numpy<2` and `pillow`; never hand-edit the binary.
