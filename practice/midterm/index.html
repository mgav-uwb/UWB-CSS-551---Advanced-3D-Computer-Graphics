  4. 4. A renderer must deliver 60 frames per second at 3840 × 2160 pixels. If one frame's time were split evenly over its pixels, how many nanoseconds would each pixel get?The big picture
    • A. 4340.28 ns
    • B. 120.56 ns
    • C. 2.01 ns
    • D. 7716.05 ns
    Answer

    C. One frame lasts 1/60 s = 16666667 ns; 3840 × 2160 = 8,294,400 pixels; 16666667 / 8,294,400 = 2.01 ns.

    A (4340.28 ns): split the frame across columns instead of pixels
    B (120.56 ns): split one whole second, not one frame, across the pixels
    D (7716.05 ns): split the frame across rows instead of pixels

  7. 7. For a = (−2, 4, −2) and b = (1, −1, −2), what is cos θ, the cosine of the angle between them? Use |a| = √24 ≈ 4.899 and |b| = √6 ≈ 2.449.Vectors

  8. 8. A projectile travels along d = (1, −3, −3) and hits a wall with unit normal n = (0, 0.8, 0.6). What is its direction after a mirror bounce (the reflection, as in HW2)?Vectors

    • C. (1, 3.72, 2.04)
    • D. (1, −9.72, −8.04)
    Answer

    C. d·n = −4.2; r = d − 2(d·n)n = (1, −3, −3) − −8.4·n = (1, 3.72, 2.04).

    D ((1, −9.72, −8.04)): added the normal term: the projectile goes further into the wall

  12. 12. A triangle of area 4 is transformed by the linear map with rows [−1, −3] and [−3, −1]. What is the area of the image?Affine transformations

  25. 25. A unit mass on a spring (k = 25, so ω = 5) is stepped with explicit Euler, Δt = 0.02. Each step multiplies the oscillation's amplitude by √(1 + ω²Δt²). By what factor has the amplitude grown after 100 steps? (1.01^50 = 1.645; 1.01^100 = 2.705)Physics simulation
    • A. 2.7
    • B. 1.64
    • C. 1
    • D. 1.5
    Answer

    B. Per step × √(1 + 0.01); after 100 steps (1 + 0.01)^(100/2) = 1.645.

    A (2.7): dropped the square root: (1 + ω²Δt²) per step
    C (1): semi-implicit Euler keeps the amplitude bounded; explicit Euler does not
    D (1.5): added the growth per step instead of compounding it
