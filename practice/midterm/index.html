  1. 1. A grid mesh built as in HW5 has n = 3 cells per side, two triangles per cell, and an index buffer of triangle-vertex indices. How many indices does the index buffer hold?Meshes
    • A. 48
    • B. 54
    • C. 96

    Answer

    B. 3² cells × 2 triangles × 3 indices = 54; the grid has 16 vertices.

    C (96): counted n + 1 cells per side

  4. 4. A renderer must deliver 30 frames per second at 1280 × 720 pixels. If one frame's time were split evenly over its pixels, how many nanoseconds would each pixel get?The big picture
    • A. 1085.07 ns
    • B. 46296.3 ns
    • C. 26041.67 ns
    • D. 36.17 ns
    Answer

    D. One frame lasts 1/30 s = 33333333 ns; 1280 × 720 = 921,600 pixels; 33333333 / 921,600 = 36.17 ns.

    A (1085.07 ns): split one whole second, not one frame, across the pixels
    B (46296.3 ns): split the frame across rows instead of pixels
    C (26041.67 ns): split the frame across columns instead of pixels

  5. 5. The text is right-handed; Unity is left-handed, and the course converts a vector between them by negating z. A student converts a = (−3, −1, 2) and b = (−2, −3, 1) to Unity, calls Vector3.Cross (the same component formula as the text), and converts the result back. What vector comes back?The loop, MVC, input, and the tool
    • A. (−5, 1, −7)
    • B. (5, −1, −7)
    • C. (−5, 1, 7)
    • D. (5, −1, 7)
    Answer

    A. Converted: a′ = (−3, −1, −2), b′ = (−2, −3, −1); a′ × b′ = (−5, 1, 7); negating z gives (−5, 1, −7), which is −(a × b): a mirror flips the cross product.

    B ((5, −1, −7)): forgot to convert the result back to the text's frame
    C ((−5, 1, 7)): negated the wrong components
    D ((5, −1, 7)): assumed the cross product is unaffected by the change of handedness

  6. 6. A 640 × 480 image has pixel (0, 0) at the top-left and rows counting down. Picking casts a ray through the center of pixel (159, 359). What are its normalized device coordinates (x, y), with NDC y pointing up?Interaction in 3D (NDC, picking, arcball)
    • A. (0.249, 0.749)
    • B. (−0.502, 0.498)
    • C. (−0.502, −0.498)
    • D. (−0.335, −0.123)
    Answer

    C. x = 2(159 + 0.5)/640 − 1 = −0.502; y = 1 − 2(359 + 0.5)/480 = −0.498.

    B ((−0.502, 0.498)): did not flip y: rows count down, NDC y counts up

  7. 7. Triangle P0 = (4, 6), P1 = (6, 1), P2 = (0, 0) in the plane. What are the barycentric weights (w0, w1, w2) of X = (2, 1), where X = w0 P0 + w1 P1 + w2 P2?Vectors
    • A. (2, 4, 10)
    • B. (0.625, 0.125, 0.25)
    • C. (0.125, 0.25, 0.625)
    • D. (0.25, 0.625, 0.125)
    Answer

    C. Total area 16; each weight is the area of the sub-triangle opposite its vertex over the total: (0.125, 0.25, 0.625).

    A ((2, 4, 10)): left the sub-triangle areas undivided by the total area
    B ((0.625, 0.125, 0.25)): paired the sub-triangle areas with the vertices in the wrong order
    D ((0.25, 0.625, 0.125)): gave each vertex the area of the sub-triangle that contains it, not the one opposite it

  8. 8. Drop a point straight onto a plane (the shadow computation, as in HW2). The plane passes through Q = (0, −1, 0) with normal m = (2, 1, 2), |m| = 3. Where does P = (−2, 3, 3) land when projected straight onto the plane?Vectors
    • A. (−6, 1, −1)
    • B. (−3.333, 2.333, 1.667)
    • C. (−0.667, 3.667, 4.333)
    • D. (−14, −3, −9)
    Answer

    B. n = m/3 = (0.667, 0.333, 0.667); s = n·(P − Q) = 2; foot = P − s n = (−3.333, 2.333, 1.667).

    A ((−6, 1, −1)): normalized the distance but stepped along the unnormalized m
    C ((−0.667, 3.667, 4.333)): moved along +n instead of back toward the plane
    D ((−14, −3, −9)): used the unnormalized m in both the distance and the direction

  9. 9. A rotation of 30° about the x axis is followed by a rotation of 30° about the y axis. What is the scalar part w of the single quaternion equal to the composition? Use: cos 15° = 0.966, sin 15° = 0.259; cos 30° = 0.866, sin 30° = 0.5.Rotation
    • A. 0.75
    • B. 0.067
    • C. 0.866
    • D. 0.933
    Answer

    D. q_x = (sin 15°, 0, 0, cos 15°), q_y = (0, sin 15°, 0, cos 15°); the scalar part of q_y q_x is w_y w_x − v_y·v_x = cos 15° cos 15° − 0 = 0.933.

    A (0.75): used the full angles instead of the half angles
    B (0.067): multiplied the vector parts instead
    C (0.866): added the angles as if both rotations were about one axis

  10. 10. Rotate v = (2, 2, −1) by 30° about the unit axis k = (1, 0, 0) (right-hand rule). Use: cos 30° = 0.866, sin 30° = 0.5.Rotation
    • A. (2, 2.232, 0.134)
    • B. (1.732, 2.232, 0.134)
    • C. (0, 2.232, 0.134)
    • D. (2, 1.232, −1.866)
    Answer

    A. along = (v·k)k = (2, 0, 0); across = (0, 2, −1); k × v = (0, 1, 2); v′ = along + cos θ·across + sin θ·(k × v) = (2, 2.232, 0.134).

    B ((1.732, 2.232, 0.134)): scaled all of v by cos θ instead of only the part across the axis
    C ((0, 2.232, 0.134)): dropped the part along the axis, which a rotation leaves unchanged
    D ((2, 1.232, −1.866)): rotated by −θ (left-hand rule): the sign of the k × v term

  15. 15. An OpenGL-style perspective matrix (camera looks down −z, NDC depth from −1 at the near plane to +1 at the far plane) has near = 1 and far = 5. What are P[2][2] and P[2][3], the entries that map view depth to clip z?Viewing
    • A. (−1.5, −1.25)
    • B. (1.5, 2.5)
    • C. (−1.5, −2.5)
    • D. (−1.5, 2.5)
    Answer

    C. P[2][2] = (f + n)/(n − f) = −1.5; P[2][3] = 2fn/(n − f) = −2.5.

    A ((−1.5, −1.25)): dropped the factor 2 in the constant term
    B ((1.5, 2.5)): wrote the denominators as f − n: flips the sign, the +z-forward convention
    D ((−1.5, 2.5)): mixed the two sign conventions

  20. 20. A mesh has V = 1000, E = 2994, F = 1996 triangles. 100 interior edge collapses are applied (each merges an edge's two endpoints and removes the two triangles that share that edge). What are V, E, F afterwards?Meshes
    • A. V = 900, E = 2794, F = 1896
    • B. V = 900, E = 2694, F = 1796
    • C. V = 900, E = 2894, F = 1796
    • D. V = 800, E = 2694, F = 1796
    Answer

    B. Each collapse: −1 vertex, −2 triangles, −3 edges (the edge itself and one of each pair of side edges), keeping V − E + F.

    A (V = 900, E = 2794, F = 1896): removed one triangle per collapse
    C (V = 900, E = 2894, F = 1796): removed only the collapsed edge; the two side edges of each removed triangle also merge
    D (V = 800, E = 2694, F = 1796): removed both endpoints; the merged vertex remains

  24. 24. A two-link arm in the plane has a shoulder at the origin, an upper link of length 1 and a forearm of length 1. Inverse kinematics must put the tip at (1.2, 1.2).     • A. 45°

    • C. 28.4°
    • D. 116.1°
    Answer

    B. Law of cosines: cos θ₂ = (d² − l1² − l2²) / (2·l1·l2) = (2.88 − 1 − 1) / 2 = 0.44, θ₂ = 63.9° (elbow down; −θ₂ is the elbow-up answer).

    C (28.4°): dropped the 2 in the denominator 2·l1·l2
